Doppler Shop privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

What the app keeps

Doppler Shop keeps a shop's products, stock, sales and debts on the shop's own phone. Debt records can include the names of the shop's customers, typed in by the shopkeeper.

What our server keeps

  • The shop name and the phone number used to sign up.
  • A scrambled copy of the shop PIN (the PIN itself is never stored).
  • The dates of the free month and of paid time.
  • If the shop connects Google Drive: the Google account email and a permission key from Google, stored locked (encrypted).

Google Drive backups

When a shop connects Google Drive, backups of the shop's records are saved in that shop's own Google Drive, in a folder called "Doppler Shop backups". The app can only see and change the files it creates there. It cannot see anything else in the Drive. The shop's records pass through our server on the way to Drive and are not kept on the server.

A shop can disconnect at any time from its Google account settings (Security, then Third-party apps with account access).

Delivery notes and receipts

When a shop uploads a delivery note, invoice, receipt or stock sheet, it is sent through our server to Cloudflare's AI service, which lists the products on it. The shopkeeper checks the list before anything is added. The file and the list are not kept on our server.

What we never do

We do not sell or share shop records or customer names, and we do not use them for advertising.
